# John Wick ("The Baba Yaga")

**Category:** Pop Culture & Action  
**Color:** Tactical Black / Gunmetal Grey (`#18191c` / `#2b2d31`)  
**Role:** Tactical Gun-Fu Master, Anti-Ranged & Brutal CQC Executioner  

---

## Lore / Background
Known in the criminal underworld as *The Baba Yaga*—the man you send to kill the Boogeyman—John Wick enters the arena driven by pure focus, commitment, and sheer will. A master of Center Axis Relock (C.A.R.) Gun-Fu, close-quarters grappling, and lethal improvisation, he turns any environment—and even the simplest stationery—into an inescapable death trap while shrugging off ballistics in his custom armored Continental suit.

## Abilities & Mechanics

### 🛡️ Passive 1: Ballistic Tailored Suit
The bespoke Kevlar lining of John's Continental suit grants specialized full-body protection against all incoming combat threats:
* **40% Damage Resistance:** Takes 40% reduced damage from all incoming enemy attacks—including close-quarters martial punches, blade slashes, kicks, enemy bullets, arrows, energy bolts, and ranged projectiles.
* **Armor Deflection:** Incoming attacks spark off his reinforced Kevlar weave, triggering a high-tech carbon-fiber grid shimmer and deflection audio.

### ⚡ Passive 2: Focus, Commitment & Sheer Will
* **Focus Meter:** Dealing damage or executing perfect combat rolls fills his **Focus Gauge**:
  * **At 100% Focus:** Gains **+25% movement speed**, instant magazine reload, and guaranteed critical strikes on close-range shots.
* **Sheer Will (Last Stand):** Upon taking fatal damage, John does not instantly fall. He triggers a **1.2s Bullet-Time Stasis** (paralyzing nearby enemies). Landing an execution shot during this window restores **15% max HP** and keeps him in the fight (once per life).

---

### 🔫 Basic Attack: Dynamic Gun-Fu (Ranged + CQC Mozambique Drill)

John dynamically shifts combat style based on distance to his target:

* **Mid/Long Range (> 70px) — TTI Pit Viper Precision Semi-Auto:**
  * Fires rapid, high-velocity semi-automatic rounds with pinpoint accuracy.
  * **Magazine System (12 Rounds):** Features tactical speed reloads (`0.6s`) when the clip is emptied, with fluid hand chamber-check animations.
* **Close Range (≤ 70px) — CQC Takedown & Mozambique Drill:**
  * Seamlessly transitions into a brutal 3-hit Gun-Fu combo:
    * **Hit 1:** Forward palm/guard thrust (90° frontal arc knockback).
    * **Hit 2:** Arm trap & close-range body grapple (stuns target briefly).
    * **Hit 3 (Finisher):** Point-blank double-tap to the chest and head with heavy screen shake, bonus critical damage, and armor puncture.

---

### 🔄 Skill 1: Tactical Combat Roll & Counter-Burst
* **Cooldown:** 5.5s  
* **Mechanic:**
  * John performs a swift **tactical combat roll / slide** in his direction of motion.
  * Grants **invulnerability frames (i-frames)** that pass directly through incoming projectiles and melee swings.
  * **Instant Counter-Shot:** Snaps aim to the nearest opponent upon rolling up, immediately firing an empowered **Armor-Piercing Round** that knocks the target backward and breaks active guards.

---

### ✏️ Skill 2: "With a F***ing Pencil" (Target-Lock Assassination Grab)
* **Cooldown:** 8.0s (With instant kill-reset mechanic)  
* **Range:** 180px Lunge Grab  
* **Mechanic & 3-Hit Puncture Sequence:**
  1. **Ambush Lunge (Gap Closer):** John dashes forward (180px) in a flash, grabs the opponent's collar/guard, and disarms their active posture.
  2. **Hit 1 — Ear / Temple Puncture (Silence & Interrupt):** Drives the reverse-grip pencil into the side of the target's head. **Instantly cancels any skill/ultimate the enemy is channeling** and applies a 1.2s `"Dazed / Ringing Ear"` silence debuff.
  3. **Hit 2 — Throat / Sternum Thrust (True Damage):** Drives a second brutal strike into the throat/chest, bypassing 100% of physical armor, shields, and damage reduction.
  4. **Hit 3 — Final Drive & Snap (Wall Slam & Arterial Bleed):** Slams the pencil fully home with his palm until the graphite snaps, violently flinging the target backward into a wall bounce. Inflicts **Deep Arterial Bleed** (heavy true damage ticking over 4.0s).
* **"Three Men in a Bar" Kill Reset:** If the pencil strike or its bleeding effect eliminates an enemy fighter or clone, **its cooldown instantly resets to 0s**, allowing John to immediately chain the execution into the next target!
* **Visual & Audio FX:** Yellow No. 2 pencil in icepick grip, sharp blood splatter bursts, graphite splinter particles, sickening puncture sound effects + wooden snap, and a floating **`"WITH A PENCIL!"`** pop-up.

### 🎬 Ultimate Skill: Excommunicado / Continental Execution (M4 Rifle Ascension)
* **Trigger:** Reaching and equipping the **M4A1 Carbine Rifle** (the pinnacle of John's Continental arsenal).
* **Ascension Multipliers & Combat Overdrive:**
  1. **🛡️ 1.5x DEF Multiplier (60% Total Damage Resistance):** Reinforced Ballistic Tailored Suit DEF increases from 40% up to **60% total damage mitigation** against all incoming combat attacks (melee, CQC, and ranged).
  2. **⚡ 1.5x Evade Multiplier / Guaranteed Evasion:** Grants guaranteed 100% tactical evasion (`EXCOMMUNICADO EVADE!`) with gold energy spark dissipation.
  3. **💨 +40% Movement Speed Multiplier (1.40x):** Movement speed accelerates from 5.4 up to **7.56 px/frame**, allowing John to blitz and reposition around the arena with supersonic momentum.
  4. **🎯 1.5x Extended Ammo Capacity (45 Rounds):** High-capacity 45-round extended PMAG drum (up from 30) loaded with supersonic 5.56x45mm NATO armor-piercing rounds.
  5. **🔥 Rapid 7-Frame Rate of Fire:** High-velocity automatic rifle spray (~8.5 rounds/second) with compensator jet shockwaves, gold tracer trails, and kinetic pushback.

## Matchup Dynamics

* **Strengths:**
  * **Anti-Ranged & Bullet Hell:** Heavily counters projectile-heavy fighters (GunSlinger, Laser, Layla, Sharpshooter) using his Ballistic Suit and Roll i-frames.
  * **Channel Interruption:** Completely shuts down long-cast channelers (Gojo preparing Purple, Genos charging Core Blast) with the instant-interrupt Pencil grab.
  * **Brawler Disruption:** Punishes aggressive rushdown fighters with point-blank CQC grapples, shotgun knockback, and pencil True Damage.
* **Weaknesses:**
  * **Spatial & Domain Paralyzers:** Susceptible to non-projectile spatial control (e.g., Gojo's Unlimited Void, Sukuna's Malevolent Shrine) if unable to roll or break out in time.
  * **Magazine Downtime:** Vulnerable during speed reload intervals if his combat roll is on cooldown.
