# Uryu Ishida (The Last Quincy)

**Category:** Anime & Fantasy  
**Color:** Quincy Radiant Cyan / Pure Silver (`#00E5FF`, `#E0F7FA`)  
**Role:** Tactical Ranged Marksman, Spatial Trapper, Damage Reversal Specialist  

## Lore / Background
The Last Quincy and successor to the Wandenreich throne, Uryu Ishida, steps into the arena. Unlike Soul Reapers who cut souls with swords, Uryu manipulates ambient spirit particles (Reishi) to manifest bows, precision arrows, and ancient Quincy artifacts. A brilliant combat tactician, Uryu excels at maintaining spatial distance, setting geometric traps, and wielding his terrifying Schrift **"A" — The Antithesis** to reverse dire situations in an instant.

## Abilities & Mechanics

### 🛡️ Passive 1: Reishi Absorption & Sklaverei Gauge
As a Quincy master, Uryu does not generate spirit energy from within—he weaponizes and absorbs the ambient energy of the battlefield.
* **Sklaverei Reishi Gauge:** When nearby enemies fire projectiles, channel domain expansions (like *Malevolent Shrine* or *Unlimited Void*), or when Uryu blocks attacks, he absorbs their ambient Reishi into his gauge (displayed above his HP bar).
* **Piercing Light State:** At 100% Reishi Gauge, Uryu enters *Piercing Light* for 6 seconds:
  * His spirit arrows gain +35% velocity, pierce through multiple enemies/illusions simultaneously, and ignore 30% of target damage reduction.
  * Bow drawing cooldowns are reduced by 40%.

### 🧵 Passive 2: Ransōtengai (Heavenly Wild Puppet Suit)
An ancient high-level Quincy technique that allows the user to manipulate their own body with spirit particle marionette lines, ignoring paralysis, shattered bones, and fatal exhaustion.
* **Emergency Activation:** Automatically triggers when Uryu's health drops below **30% HP** or when inflicted with heavy crowd control (knockdowns, non-time-stop stuns).
* **Puppet Stance:** Glowing cyan Reishi strings visibly bind his limbs. Instantly purges active stuns/slows, grants **+45% Movement Speed**, and renders him immune to physical flinch and weapon knockback for 6.0 seconds.

---

### 🏹 Basic Attack: Heilig Bogen (Ginrei Kojaku) & Seele Schneider
Uryu dynamically switches between long-range spirit arrow volleys and close-quarters blade parries depending on opponent spacing.

* **Long Range (>70px): Heilig Pfeil (Sacred Arrows)**
  * Uryu draws his glowing cyan Reishi bow, firing rapid bursts of **3 to 5 pinpoint Heilig Pfeil arrows** in a tight supersonic trajectory.
  * Arrows travel at high velocity (Speed 22), dealing piercing damage and applying micro-knockback.
  * Leaves a clean trailing cyan needle streak with a crisp glass-shatter impact sound.
* **Close Quarters (<70px): Seele Schneider Intercept**
  * If an enemy rushes into point-blank melee range, Uryu instantly draws **Seele Schneider** (the vibrating spirit sword).
  * Executes a rapid **120° frontal arc parry slash** vibrating at 3 million rotations per second. Cuts through incoming projectiles, deals shred damage, and pushes the attacker back to reset spacing.

---

### 💨 Skill 1: Hirenkyaku (Flying Screen Step) & Licht Regen
A combination of instantaneous Reishi glide mobility and a devastating arrow downpour.

* **Hirenkyaku (Glide Step):**
  * Uryu instantly glides across spirit particle platforms on the ground away from incoming threats, gaining **0.3s of evasion/i-frames**.
  * Leaves 3 cyan manga afterimages with backward-streaming 4-point needle speed lines (`#00E5FF`, `#00B0FF`, `#FFFFFF`, `#0A192F`).
* **Licht Regen (Rain of Light Barrage):**
  * Immediately following Hirenkyaku (or upon reactivation), Uryu aims his bow skyward and unleashes **Licht Regen**: a concentrated ceiling volley of over **100 micro-arrows** raining down into the target zone.
  * Traps caught opponents in rapid hit-sparks and flinch, shredding shields and suppressing dashes.

---

### ✡️ Skill 2: Gintō Setup: Sprenger (Quincy Pentagram Trap)
A devastating spatial trap utilizing five spirit stakes and condensed silver-tube liquid Reishi.

* **Setup Phase:**
  * Uryu fires **5 Seele Schneider stakes** into the ground encircling the targeted opponent in a wide geometric 5-pointed Quincy Pentagram formation.
* **Detonation Phase:**
  * Uryu tosses a **Gintō (Silver Tube)** into the pentagram center (*"A bank of quartz, the will of steel... Haizen!"*).
  * The pentagram ignites, erupting into a **massive ascending pillar of radiant cyan Reishi energy** dealing heavy true damage to everything inside while vaporizing all incoming projectiles.

---

### ⚡ Ultimate Skill: Quincy: Vollständig & Schrift "A" — The Antithesis
Pushes Uryu's Quincy lineage to its ultimate zenith, combining celestial holy form with reality-inverting hax.

* **Phase 1: Vollständig Awakening**
  * Uryu sprouts a grand, radiant **asymmetric Reishi wing** of pure cyan light and a rotating Quincy halo (*Kugelfang*) above his head.
  * Arrows upgrade into heavy beam railguns, and his movement speed is permanently boosted during the form.
* **Phase 2: Schrift "A" — The Antithesis (Complete State Reversal)**
  * When activated (or triggered upon receiving lethal damage), Uryu designates two events between himself and his chosen opponent.
  * Spatial Quincy crosses lock onto both combatants, and **The Antithesis reverses their exact health and damage states**:
    $$\text{State (Uryu)} \longleftrightarrow \text{State (Opponent)}$$
  * If Uryu is critically wounded at **15% HP** and his opponent is healthy at **90% HP**, their health values instantly swap—plunging the enemy into near-death while fully restoring Uryu!

## Fighter Interactions & Lore Synergy

### ⚔️ vs Ichigo Kurosaki (Fated Rivalry)
As long-time allies and rivals, their combat styles contrast sharply—Ichigo's raw close-range physical pressure versus Uryu's calculated long-range precision.
* **Matchup Dynamic:** When *Getsuga Tensho* collides with *Licht Regen*, a special clash sound effect triggers with radial manga speed lines. Ichigo's *Shunpo* tests Uryu's *Hirenkyaku* reflexes.

### 🕶️ vs Satoru Gojo (Quincy Precision vs Limitless Infinity)
* **Matchup Dynamic:** Standard *Heilig Pfeil* arrows are blocked and suspended by Gojo's **Limitless Infinity barrier**. However, Uryu's **The Antithesis** completely bypasses Infinity, because it does not travel through space—it conceptually inverts the casualty states between the two fighters.

### 👿 vs Ryomen Sukuna (Reishi Dominance vs Malevolent Shrine)
* **Matchup Dynamic:** When Sukuna unleashes *Malevolent Shrine*, Uryu activates **Ransōtengai** to maintain full combat mobility while his **Sklaverei Gauge** hyper-charges from the storm of ambient cursed energy slashes, allowing him to fire super-charged *Piercing Light* arrows through the domain.

### 🗡️ vs Toji Fushiguro (Mage Hunter vs Pure Distance)
* **Matchup Dynamic:** Toji's stealth ambush and Inverted Spear nullify barriers, making him lethal up close. Uryu must rely heavily on *Seele Schneider* parry knockbacks and *Hirenkyaku* spacing to keep Toji out of his deadly melee kill-zone.

### ☸️ vs Mahoraga (The Adaptation Problem)
* **Matchup Dynamic:** Mahoraga gradually adapts to Uryu's basic *Heilig Pfeil* shots after repeated hits. To secure victory, Uryu must alter his damage profile using *Sprenger's* pentagram true damage or finish Mahoraga instantly with *The Antithesis*.

* **HUD Bar Consistency (Rule 18):** All skill progress bars in the HUD use unified Quincy Cyan (`#00E5FF`) styling.
