# Yuta Okkotsu

**Category:** Anime  
**Role:** Defensive Counter / Tank / Summoner  

## Overview
A highly resilient Special Grade sorcerer built around defense and sustain. Yuta uses passive regeneration (RCT), parry counters, and teleport dodges to absorb and negate enemy assaults while Rika provides heavy defensive cover.

## Abilities
- **RCT Regeneration (Passive):** Continuous health recovery with a 1-time emergency revival on fatal damage.
- **Parry & Teleport Dodge:** Invulnerable parry stance that deflects attacks, paired with evasive teleportation.
- **Rika Orimoto (Summon):** Autonomous cursed spirit companion offering heavy frontline cover and counter-attacks.
- **Domain Expansion:** Plunges the arena into a domain that dramatically speeds up technique cooldowns.
