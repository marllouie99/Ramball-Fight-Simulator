## Lore / Background
Straight outta Ganton, Los Santos, Carl "CJ" Johnson returned to Grove Street to bury his mother and ended up taking over the entire state of San Andreas. Armed with boundless street smarts, custom brass knuckles, and the most legendary arsenal of cheat codes in gaming history, CJ bends reality itself whenever things get out of hand. Whether he is dropping `$250,000` greenbacks to patch his bulletproof vest (`HESOYAM`), rocketing through the stratosphere on a stolen Area 69 jetpack (`ROCKETMAN`), summoning a homie drive-by (`GROVESTREET4LIFE`), or walking through explosions in God Mode with a Minigun (`BAGUVIX`), CJ proves that Grove Street is always King.

## Abilities & Mechanics

### 🛡️ Passive 1: `RESPECT+` & `HEAT GAUGE`
* **Respect Meter (0–100%):**
  * Landing brass knuckle punches and skill attacks fills CJ's **Respect Bar**.
  * **At 50% Respect:** Gains **+15% Movement Speed** and **+10% Punch Knockback**.
  * **At 100% Respect ("Grove Street OG"):** Gains **+25% Attack Speed**, **10% Damage Resistance**, and reduces all active skill cooldowns by **1.5s**.
* **Cheat Code Dialer:**
  * Every ability activation flashes the nostalgic retro **`[ CHEAT ACTIVATED: <CODE> ]`** HUD notification with the authentic San Andreas chime SFX, raining green dollar bill motes (`$ $ $`).

### 👊 Basic Attack: Brass Knuckles Street Boxing
* **Reach:** `80 px` | **Frontal Arc:** `120°` (Rule 8 Multi-Target Arc Compliant) | **Cooldown:** `18 frames (~0.30s)` | **Damage:** `24`
* **Mechanic & Combo Flow:**
  * CJ alternates between heavy left lead jabs and thunderous right crosses with metallic brass knuckles.
  * **Multi-Target Frontal Arc:** Cleaves through all enemy entities and illusions standing inside the 120° frontal punch cone.
  * **Street Stagger:** Every 3rd consecutive hit lands a heavy **Liver Hook** that inflicts a brief `0.35s` stagger stun and launches the opponent backward with kinetic screen shake and comic metallic impact sparks.

### 💰 Skill 1: `HESOYAM` (Health, Kevlar Armor & $250k Cash Shockwave)
* **Cooldown:** `7.0s`
* **Mechanic:**
  * CJ taps in `H-E-S-O-Y-A-M`:
    1. **Armor & Health Surge:** Instantly restores **60 HP** and equips a **75 HP Bulletproof Kevlar Shield** that absorbs incoming damage before draining real health.
    2. **$250,000 Cash Blast (AOE Shockwave):** Emits an explosive burst of flying green banknotes and golden coins in a `160px` radius around CJ, dealing `35` damage and knocking all surrounding enemies backward.
    3. **Kevlar Deflection:** While the armor shield remains active, incoming basic attacks trigger metallic ricochet sparks.

---

### 🚀 Skill 2: `ROCKETMAN` / `YECGAA` (DARPA Jetpack Flight & Strafe)
* **Duration:** `4.5s` | **Cooldown:** `9.5s`
* **Mechanic:**
  * CJ summons the Area 69 Jetpack, locking it onto his back with roaring rocket thruster flames.
  * **Omni-Directional Hover Flight:** Increases movement speed to **`7.8 px/frame`** (1.30x) with zero ground friction, allowing 360° fluid strafing and dodging through enemy attacks.
  * **Incendiary Thruster Burn:** Twin exhaust flames shoot downward and backward behind CJ, igniting the ground in fiery patches that deal burning damage to any enemy pursuing directly behind him.
  * **Airborne Knuckle Dives:** Performing a punch while airborne causes CJ to rocket-boost forward in a high-speed supersonic punch dive (`110px` dash).

---

### 🚗 Skill 3: `GROVESTREET4LIFE` (Gang Drive-By Backup)
* **Cooldown:** `10.0s`
* **Mechanic:**
  * CJ whistles for backup. A midnight-green **Grove Street Greenwood sedan** screeches into the arena boundary in a cloud of burning rubber smoke!
  * **Drive-By Lead Storm:** Two Grove Street homies in green bandanas lean out the windows and spray a rapid 20-round burst of high-velocity **Tec-9 / Micro SMG** tracer fire directly at CJ's target, dealing rapid kinetic damage and heavily staggering the enemy.
  * **Tire Burnout Exit:** The car performs a high-speed power slide before speeding off, leaving a burning oil patch that slows opponents who step into it.

---

### 👑 Ultimate Skill: `BAGUVIX` (God Mode & Minigun Riot Overdrive)
* **Duration:** `5.0s` | **Requirement:** Full Ultimate Energy
* **Mechanic & Overdrive State:**
  * CJ enters full **God Mode (`BAGUVIX`)**:
    1. **100% Invulnerability Shield:** Gains a glowing golden/emerald matrix code aura that absorbs 100% of all incoming damage and immunizes CJ to hit-stuns, freezes, and knockbacks.
    2. **M134 Heavy Minigun Equipped:** CJ whips out a six-barrel rotating Minigun with **`FULLCLIP` Infinite Ammo**, unleashing a devastating `45 rounds/sec` barrage of supersonic armor-piercing bullets.
    3. **Riot Mode Shockwaves:** Every second of sustained firing triggers an expanding fiery pressure wave that pushes all arena entities back toward the walls.
  * Upon expiring, CJ drops the overheated minigun barrels in a cloud of steam with the iconic line: *"Grove Street is King!"*

## Matchup Dynamics

* **Strengths:**
  * **Survivability & Reset:** `HESOYAM` gives CJ one of the best on-demand healing and shielding tools in the game, allowing him to trade blows aggressively.
  * **Superior Aerial Mobility:** `ROCKETMAN` allows CJ to evade ground-based traps, closed-barrier attacks, and out-kite melee specialists.
  * **Overwhelming Pressure:** `GROVESTREET4LIFE` drive-by and `BAGUVIX` Minigun completely pin opponents down with immense hit-pause and projectile volume.
* **Weaknesses:**
  * **Timing Vulnerability:** Outside of `BAGUVIX`, CJ lacks native passive armor reduction and must actively manage his `HESOYAM` shield uptime.
