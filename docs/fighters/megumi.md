# Megumi Fushiguro

**Category:** Anime & Fantasy  
**Color:** Midnight Navy / Deep Indigo (`#1c2d4a`)  
**Role:** Tactical Shadow Summoner, Mobile Ambush Specialist, High-Stakes Neutralizer  

---

## Lore / Background
The prodigy bearer of the Zen'in clan's prized **Ten Shadows Technique** (*Tokusa no Kage Bōjutsu*), Megumi Fushiguro uses shadows as intermediaries to summon a versatile arsenal of Shikigami and manipulate physical space. Unlike standard brute-force sorcerers, Megumi excels at calculated tactical combat, shadow stealth, and multi-angle ambush tactics. When pushed past his mortal limits, he holds the ultimate suicide trump card: invoking the untamed subjugation ritual of the unvanquished Divine General Mahoraga.

---

## Abilities & Mechanics

### 🌑 Passive: Liquid Shadow Reservoir & Shadow Sink (*Kage no Utsuwa*)
Megumi manipulates the ground as a fluid shadow reservoir, granting him fluid battlefield mobility and evasion:
* **Shadow Sink Evasion (Dash):** Whenever Megumi dashes or evades, he submerges completely into the arena floor as a flat liquid shadow pool for **0.6s**. While submerged, he is fully intangible and can glide directly underneath enemies and projectiles.
* **Shadow Blade Ambush:** Re-emerging behind an enemy delivers an instant shadow-infused cursed dagger strike, afflicting the target with flinch and a **35% movement slow for 1.2s**.
* **Shadow Storage:** Enemy projectiles passing over Megumi's shadow puddles are absorbed into his shadow reservoir, charging his Cursed Energy gauge.

---

### 🗡️ Basic Attack: Cursed Dagger & Shadow Martial Arts
* **Frontal Arc Slash:** Performs fluid close-range slashes with a cursed dagger across a **120° frontal arc** with dark indigo shadow trail effects.
* **Physical Push & Flinch:** Strikes deal sharp physical damage, stagger the opponent, and apply subtle knockback.

---

### 🐺 Skill 1: Divine Dog: Totality (*Kon: Zen*) — Ambush & Companion
* **Shadow Eruption:** Summons Divine Dog: Totality directly out of the opponent’s shadow.
* **Savage Twin-Claw Flurry:** Totality lunges forward across a wide frontal arc, delivering twin-claw lacerations dealing **heavy bleed damage** and physical wall-bounce knockback.
* **Active Autonomous Companion:** Totality stays on the battlefield as a mobile ally (similar to Rika or Doppelganger clones), chasing down targets, biting, and peeling for Megumi.

---

### ⚡ Skill 2: Nue (*Thunder Bird*) & Toad (*Gama*) — Aerial Stun & Tether
* **Nue (Electric Dive-Bomb):** Manifests the winged beast Nue from the sky to execute an electrified dive-bomb. Strikes trigger an **Electric Stun (`electricStunTimer`)**, interrupting active skill channeling and paralyzing the target with crackling lightning.
* **Toad (Shadow Tongue Pull):** Casts shadow toads that extend long adhesive tongues across the arena to latch onto enemies, dragging them into Megumi's melee range or interrupting runaway targets.

---

### 🐘 Skill 3: Max Elephant (*Bansho*) & Rabbit Escape (*Datsuto*)
* **Max Elephant (Crushing Weight & Torrent Flood):** Drops a gargantuan shadow elephant directly from above, crushing the impact area with massive screen shake and unleashing a pressurized tidal wave that washes enemies into arena boundaries.
* **Rabbit Escape (Decoy Smokescreen):** Discharges a swarm of hundreds of shadow rabbits across the field. The rabbits act as a living shield, soaking enemy projectiles, confusing auto-aim, and granting Megumi a **+40% movement speed boost**.

---

### 🌌 Ultimate / Domain Expansion: Chimera Shadow Garden (*Kango An'eitei*)
Because Megumi's domain expansion is **incomplete and open-barrier**, it transforms the physical arena into a chaotic shadow realm:
* **Liquid Shadow Floor:** The entire arena floor turns into deep, boiling liquid black shadows with undulating shadow hands and mist.
* **Deep Sinking Penalty:** Trapped opponents experience heavy traction loss and a **50% movement speed reduction**.
* **Infinite Shadow Doubles:** Megumi and his Shikigami can produce endless shadow Doppelganger clones that attack simultaneously without cooldowns.
* **Coordinated Shadow Execution:** Megumi and multiple Totality clones erupt from beneath the enemy in a high-speed, multi-angle manga speed-line cross-slash combo.

---

### ⚔️ Desperation Climax: Untamed Mahoraga Subjugation Ritual (*Makora*)
> *"With this treasure, I summon... Eight-Handled Sword Divergent Sila Divine General Mahoraga!"*

When Megumi's health drops below **20% HP** (or upon activating his desperation ritual trigger), Megumi can invoke the forbidden death-gamble ritual:
* **Neutral Ritual Summon:** Spawns the existing **Divine General Mahoraga** into the arena as an **untamed neutral force of destruction**.
* **Uncontrolled Calamity:** Mahoraga is bound to nobody—he attacks **both Megumi and all opposing fighters** with his full arsenal:
  * **Sword of Extermination:** Deals True Damage and bypasses all barriers/shields.
  * **Wheel of Adaptation:** Progressively adapts to all damage phenomena received from any fighter in the arena.
* **High-Stakes Survival:** If an opponent defeats Mahoraga, the ritual ends; if Mahoraga wipes out everyone, the match is claimed by the calamity.
