# Ichigo Kurosaki

**Category:** Anime & Fantasy  
**Color:** Black / Deep Red (`#000000`, `#8b0000`)  
**Role:** Rushdown Melee, Burst Assassin  

## Lore / Background
The Substitute Soul Reaper, Ichigo Kurosaki, steps onto the battlefield. Wielding his Bankai, Tensa Zangetsu, his speed is pushed to the absolute limit. When pushed to the brink, his Inner Hollow takes over, granting him terrifying power, enhanced speed, and chaotic destruction. 

---

## Abilities

### 🛡️ Passive: Hollow Resolve & Mask Gauge
As Ichigo fights, he constantly balances his Soul Reaper abilities with his Inner Hollow.
* **Hollow Gauge:** Taking damage or landing abilities builds his Hollow Gauge.
* **Hollow Mask Awakening:** When his health drops below 30% (or when the gauge fills), he instantly dons his **Hollow Mask**. While active, he gains a massive movement speed and attack damage multiplier (`hollowSpeedMultiplier: 1.4`).
* **Enhanced Visuals:** His spiritual pressure aura turns black and red, and all his abilities upgrade to their chaotic hollow forms.

### 🗡️ Basic Attack: Tensa Zangetsu Cleave
A lightning-fast sword swing taking advantage of his Bankai's compact form.
* **Frontal Arc Sweep:** Hits multiple enemies caught in the blade's path, dealing solid damage and applying hit-stun.
* **Black Getsuga Coating:** When the Hollow Mask is active, his sword glows black and red, increasing the sweep range and damage slightly.

### 💥 Skill 1: Getsuga Tensho
Ichigo winds up and swings his blade, releasing his spiritual pressure as a crescent-shaped wave.
* **Bankai Form:** Fires a fast blue energy wave that pierces standard projectiles and damages enemies in a line.
* **Hollow Mask Form (Black Getsuga Tensho):** Upgrades into the devastating Black Getsuga. Travels much faster, deals increased True Damage, and leaves a brief red and black chaotic trail that damages enemies who walk through it. Applies hit-pause to targets caught in the blast.

### 💨 Skill 2: Flash Step (Shunpo) Assault
Using the extreme speed of his Bankai, Ichigo instantly closes the distance.
* **Supersonic Dash:** Instantly teleports or dashes toward the target (or aim direction).
* **Backstab Slash:** If he dashes through an enemy, he instantly delivers a high-speed backstab slash upon reappearing.
* **Manga Speed Lines:** Leaves behind 4-point filled needle polygons of white and deep blue (or red if hollowfied) to convey his supersonic momentum.

### 🌪️ Ultimate Skill: Vasto Lorde Awakening
Ichigo completely surrenders to his Inner Hollow, transforming into the terrifying Vasto Lorde.
* **Spiritual Pressure Shockwave:** Upon activation, a massive shockwave erupts, knocking back all enemies and destroying incoming projectiles.
* **Unstoppable Regeneration:** He becomes immune to crowd control and gains extreme high-speed health regeneration.
* **Point-Blank Cero Finisher:** At the end of the duration (or upon reactivation), he grabs the nearest target, applying a time-stop freeze to them, and fires a devastating point-blank red **Cero** from his horns. This deals massive AOE damage in a wide beam before he reverts to his base form.
