# Layla - Cosmic Marksman (Purple Fighter)

Layla is a scaling ranged fighter who grows stronger as the battle progresses. Unlike traditional fighters with fixed capabilities, Layla's power increases with each successful hit, making her a late-game threat that rewards consistent accuracy and positioning.

## Combat Mechanics

### Ascending Power (Passive)
* **Mechanic:** Progressive damage and range scaling.
* **Effect:** Each successful basic attack hit increases Layla's attack range and projectile damage.
* **Stacking:** Damage increases by 1.5 per stack, range increases by 15px per stack.
* **Maximum:** Caps at 10 stacks (max +15 damage, +150px range).
* **Visual:** Layla emits a purple glow that intensifies with each stack accumulated.
* **Reset:** Stacks reset when Layla takes damage or after 5 seconds of no hits.

### Malefic Gun (Basic Attack)
* **Mechanic:** Precision projectile fire.
* **Effect:** Fires energy projectiles that deal base damage plus passive bonus.
* **Base Damage:** 12 damage per shot.
* **Base Range:** 300px projectile range.
* **Fire Rate:** Moderate (similar to normal fighter, 70 frame cooldown).
* **Projectile Speed:** Fast but not instant (3.0x multiplier).
* **Aiming:** Uses smooth target tracking similar to Sharpshooter.

### Malefic Bomb (Skill 1)
* **Mechanic:** Skill shot AOE damage with slow effect.
* **Trigger:** Active skill activation (cooldown-based).
* **Damage:** 20 AOE damage on impact.
* **Effect:** Applies 40% movement speed slow to enemies in blast radius.
* **On-Hit Buff:** Successfully hitting an enemy grants Layla +1 movement speed for 180 frames (3 seconds).
* **Duration:** Slow lasts 90 frames (1.5 seconds).
* **Cooldown:** 180 frames (3 seconds).
* **Range:** 250px casting range.
* **Visual:** Rotating golden gear with a glowing magenta core that explodes in a cosmic blast pattern.

### Void Dash (Skill 2)
* **Mechanic:** Quick repositioning with damaging trail.
* **Trigger:** Active skill activation.
* **Dash Distance:** 80px instant displacement.
* **Trail Effect:** Leaves a purple energy trail that damages enemies.
* **Trail Damage:** 8 damage per second to enemies in the path.
* **Trail Duration:** 60 frames (1 second).
* **Cooldown:** 120 frames (2 seconds).
* **Passive Interaction:** Dash immediately resets basic attack cooldown, allowing instant follow-up shots.
* **Visual:** Purple afterimage trail with cosmic particle effects.

### Destruction Barrage (Ultimate)
* **Mechanic:** Enhanced rapid-fire mode with maximum range.
* **Trigger:** Ultimate ability activation.
* **Duration:** 120 frames (2 seconds).
* **Fire Rate:** 3x normal attack speed (23 frame cooldown between shots).
* **Damage:** 1.5x damage multiplier per shot (18 base + passive bonus).
* **Range Bonus:** +200px additional range during ultimate (total up to 650px with max stacks).
* **Cooldown:** 600 frames (10 seconds).
* **Visual:** Layla's weapon glows intensely, projectiles become larger and more luminous, screen darkens slightly with purple tint.

## Stat Profile

* **HP:** 55 (glass cannon - below average durability)
* **Move Speed:** 4.5 (slightly below average mobility)
* **Base Damage:** 12 (moderate base damage)
* **Attack Range:** 300px (good starting range, excellent with stacks)
* **Color:** Purple/Violet (#9b59b6)
* **Category:** Sci-Fi & Modern
* **Difficulty:** Medium-High (requires stack management and positioning)

## Playstyle

Layla excels at mid-to-long range engagements where she can safely build her passive stacks. Her optimal strategy involves:

1. **Early Game:** Play cautiously, using Malefic Bomb to zone enemies and build initial stacks.
2. **Mid Game:** Once stacks are built, maintain aggression while using Void Dash for repositioning and attack resets.
3. **Late Game:** With maximum stacks and Destruction Barrage available, Layla becomes a devastating ranged threat capable of shredding opponents from extreme distances.

**Weaknesses:** Low HP makes her vulnerable to rush-down fighters. Stacking mechanic requires consistent accuracy, and taking damage resets her progress.
