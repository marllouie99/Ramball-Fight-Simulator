# Yuji Itadori - The Black Flash Brawler

**Category:** Anime
**Color:** Deep Pink-Purple (`#9B1FD4`) / Crimson Vessel (`#8B0000` during Soul Swap)
**Role:** Aggressive Brawler, Black Flash Specialist

## Lore / Background
The human vessel of the King of Curses, Ryomen Sukuna. Yuji Itadori fights not with refined technique, but with an overwhelming, instinctive martial prowess amplified by cursed energy. He is capable of landing Black Flash — a near-impossible feat that distorts space itself — with terrifying frequency, making him one of the most physically dangerous humans alive. He carries the weight of Sukuna's soul inside him, and in the direst moments, that dark power bleeds through.

## Abilities

### 👊 Passive: Divergent Fist
Every punch Yuji throws carries a delayed cursed energy shockwave that detonates a split second after the initial impact. This means each strike deals **two instances of damage** — the physical hit and a trailing cursed surge that catches enemies who try to retreat immediately after being punched.

### ⚡ Core Mechanic: Black Flash Buildup
Yuji tracks the number of consecutive hits he lands on a target. After **4 successful strikes**, the next punch automatically triggers a **Black Flash** — a spatially distorted, black-and-purple void burst that deals massive bonus damage and launches the enemy with brutal knockback. The counter resets after Black Flash fires or if Yuji misses.

### 🥊 Basic Attack: Cursed Energy Punch
High-speed close-range brawling punches. Multi-target frontal arc (90°, ~65px reach). Each punch advances the Black Flash charge counter and triggers the Divergent Fist shockwave.

### 💨 Skill 1: Divergent Fist Combo Rush
Yuji dashes directly into his target and unleashes a **rapid 6-hit punch flurry**. Each hit in the combo carries the Divergent Fist shockwave, and the Black Flash charge builds through the sequence. If the charge hits 4 during the combo, Black Flash detonates mid-flurry without interrupting the remaining hits. Has a long cooldown to prevent spamming.

### 🩺 Skill 2: Reverse Cursed Technique
Yuji, drawing on the cursed energy flowing through him as Sukuna's vessel, channels a brief RCT pulse. He pauses and glows with soft pink energy, **healing a portion of his max HP**. This technique is extremely taxing and has a very long cooldown — it can only be used **once per round**. Interrupting him during the channeling cancels the heal entirely.

### 👹 Ultimate: Soul Swap — Sukuna Takes Over
When Yuji's health drops critically low, Sukuna momentarily **seizes control of the vessel**. Yuji's body erupts with crimson cursed energy and dark tattoo-like markings. For a limited duration, Sukuna's power surges through him:
- All damage is amplified significantly
- Black Flash triggers after only **2 hits** instead of 4
- Gains access to a single ranged **Dismantle slash** (borrowed from Sukuna's kit)
- Movement speed increases noticeably

When the duration expires, Yuji reclaims control but suffers a brief stagger as he reasserts his will over his own body. This transformation can only occur **once per match**.
