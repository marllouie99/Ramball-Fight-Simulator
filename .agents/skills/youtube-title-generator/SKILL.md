# YouTube Video Title & Packaging Generator Playbook

Use this skill whenever the user asks for video titles, YouTube Shorts hooks, thumbnail copy, clickbait angles, or video packaging for any fighter, matchup, or battle in *Circle Mini-Battle / Fight of Characters (FOC)*.

---

## 🎯 Core Objectives & CTR Psychology

YouTube gaming audiences (especially for YouTube Shorts, anime fighting games, and battle simulators) click based on **tension, contrast, and curiosity**:
1. **The Asymmetric Contrast**: Pit one fighter's core defining trait against the opponent's absolute extreme (e.g. A sleeping coward vs The King of the Gods; Infinite Infinity vs The Man Who Can't Use Magic; 100% Luck vs Absolute Calculation).
2. **The Curiosity Gap**: Create a question or scenario in the viewer's mind that can only be answered by watching (e.g. *"What happens when God meets Godspeed?"*).
3. **The Philosophical / Poetic Hook**: A powerful one-liner that sounds like an anime episode title or manga chapter climax (e.g. *"Zeus Commands The Thunder. Zenitsu Became It."*).
4. **The Hypersonic Shorts Hook**: All-caps, punchy, high-energy hooks with high-contrast emojis built for mobile swiping on the vertical Shorts feed.

---

## 🏛️ Standard 5-Tier Title Output Structure

Whenever a user requests video titles for a matchup or character, the agent **MUST ALWAYS** generate titles organized across these 5 distinct categories, concluding with a **Top Recommendation**:

### Tier 1: 🌌 Unique / Poetic / Thematic Contrasts *(The "Story" Hook)*
*Focus on the philosophical, lore, or thematic irony between both combatants.*
* **Formula A**: `"[Fighter A Action/Status]. [Fighter B Transcendence/Reaction]."`
  - *Example*: *"Zeus Commands The Thunder. Zenitsu Became It."*
* **Formula B**: `"What Happens When [Absolute Force] Meets [Opposite Extreme]?"`
  - *Example*: *"What Happens When A God Meets Godspeed?"*
* **Formula C**: `"The [Unexpected Archetype] Who Challenged [The Supreme Authority]..."`
  - *Example*: *"A Sleeping Coward Just Challenged The King of Olympus..."*
* **Formula D**: `"[Mythological Event]: [Character Consequence]"`
  - *Example*: *"The Sky Sent A God. The Earth Sent A Boy With A Sword."*

### Tier 2: ⚡ High-CTR YouTube Shorts Hooks *(Mobile Feed & Algorithmic Swiping)*
*Short, intense, emoji-accented, punchy hooks designed for 540×960 vertical YouTube Shorts.*
* **Formula**: `"[SHOCKING SCENARIO IN CAPS]! ⚡💀"`
  - *Example*: `WHEN ZENITSU FALLS ASLEEP AGAINST A LITERAL GOD... ⚡😴`
  - *Example*: `1,000,000 VOLTS VS HYPERSONIC THUNDERCLAP! ⚡⚔️`
  - *Example*: `DID HE JUST SPEED-BLITZ THE KING OF THE GODS?! 😱⚡`
  - *Example*: `WHO IS THE REAL GOD OF LIGHTNING? (Zeus vs Zenitsu)`

### Tier 3: 💬 Debate & Comment Drivers *(High Community Engagement)*
*Designed to make viewers debate in the comments section who would win in lore vs gameplay.*
* **Formula**: `"Can [Fighter A's Signature Ability] Actually Pierce/Stop [Fighter B's Defense]?"`
  - *Example*: *"Can Thunder Breathing Cut Through Divine Olympus Lightning?"*
  - *Example*: *"Is Zenitsu Fast Enough to Dodge Zeus's Divine Thunder Storm?"*
  - *Example*: *"Who Actually Wins? Zeus vs Zenitsu (The Ultimate Thunder Duel)"*

### Tier 4: 🗡️ Cinematic / Manga Climax One-Liners *(Atmospheric & High Retention)*
*Reads like an iconic manga chapter title or anime climax battle card.*
* **Formula**: `"[Poetic Statement]: [Fighter A] vs [Fighter B]"`
  - *Example*: *"Lightning Cannot Strike What It Cannot Catch."*
  - *Example*: *"Even Gods Bleed at Godspeed: Zenitsu vs Zeus"*
  - *Example*: *"The Day A Mortal Sliced The Thunder In Half"*
  - *Example*: *"The Boy Who Put The God of Thunder To Sleep"*

### Tier 5: 🏆 Clean Channel Branding & SEO Format *(Standard FOC Search Alignment)*
*Optimized for YouTube search indexing, playlists, and recurring series watchers.*
* **Formula**: `"[Fighter A] vs [Fighter B] | [Epic Subtitle] [Circle Mini-Battle]"`
  - *Example*: *"Zeus vs Zenitsu | The Thunder Gods Clash! [Circle Mini-Battle]"*
  - *Example*: *"ZEUS VS ZENITSU - Ultimate Lightning Showdown! ⚡ [FOC]"*
  - *Example*: *"Zeus (Olympus) vs Zenitsu (Demon Slayer) | 1v1 Battle Simulator"*

## 🚫 Anti-Patterns (What to Avoid)

1. **Never Output Only Generic "X vs Y"**:
   - ❌ Bad: *"Zeus vs Zenitsu Gameplay"*, *"Circle Mini-Battle Match 4"*.
   - ✅ Good: Always provide unique thematic contrasts, Shorts hooks, and debate angles alongside standard SEO.
2. **Avoid Misrepresenting the Game Style**:
   - The game is *Circle Mini-Battle / Fight of Characters*, known for visceral pixel art, high-speed anime combat, dynamic camera tracking, and explosive visual effects. Titles should match this adrenaline.
3. **Don't Forget the Winning Recommendation**:
   - Always single out **one standout title** as the top recommendation and explain *why* it works psychologically (e.g. why it triggers high CTR or comment debates).
