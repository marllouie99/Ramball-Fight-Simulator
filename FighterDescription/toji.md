# Toji Fushiguro

**Category:** Anime & Fantasy  
**Color:** Dark Olive / Charcoal (`#242722`)  
**Role:** Anti-Mage, Rushdown Melee Assassin  

## Lore / Background
The Sorcerer Killer, Toji Fushiguro, enters the arena. Born with a Heavenly Restriction that completely stripped him of Cursed Energy in exchange for overwhelming physical prowess, he operates outside the normal rules of combat. He is an assassin designed specifically to dismantle powerful spell-casters and dominate close-quarters engagements.

---

## Abilities

### 🛡️ Passive: Heavenly Restriction & Stealth Ambush
Having zero Cursed Energy grants Toji unparalleled physical speed and makes him invisible to domain effects and homing attacks. 
* **Domain & Homing Immunity:** Toji takes zero damage from enemy Domains (like Malevolent Shrine) and cannot be trapped or paralyzed by them (like Unlimited Void). Auto-aim projectiles ignore him.
* **Heavenly Restriction Channel Sense (30% Interrupt Counter):** When sensing an enemy within 450px actively channeling a skill or domain (like Gojo preparing Purple/Domain), Toji has a **30% chance** to immediately force-activate **Sequence 1 Ambush**, blitzing in to interrupt them!
* **3-Stage Stealth Ambush Combo:** When exiting active stealth, completing stealth cooldowns, or triggered by Channel Sense, Toji automatically executes a devastating 3-stage combo:
  * **1st Sequence (Inverted Spear Thrust):** Teleports directly into the enemy's vision, stabs them with the Inverted Spear of Heaven (nullifying barriers, cancelling skill channel, and wiping cooldowns).
  * **2nd Sequence (Split Soul Katana Execution):** Chases behind the target, holds a windup stance, and unleashes the wide **Void Purple & Pitch-Black Jagged Anime Crescent Wave**, launching the target into ricochet wall bouncing!
  * **3rd Sequence (Phantom Flurry Finale):** Toji vanishes into 6 phantom afterimages encircling the ricocheting enemy, delivering **6 rapid flash-teleport Void-Purple Crescent Slashes** (`6 × 8 = 48` True Damage) before stepping back into stealth!

### 🗡️ Basic Attack & Skill 2: Inverted Spear & Split Soul Katana
Toji fights up close using his signature cursed tool arsenal, featuring unified **Void Purple & Pitch-Black Jagged Crescent Wave** slash visuals:
* **Inverted Spear of Heaven (Skill Channel Interruption & Barrier Pierce):** Strikes ignore defensive barriers (like Gojo's Infinity). During the **1st Sequence Ambush Thrust**, if the target is actively channeling a skill or ultimate (like Gojo casting Hollow Purple/Domain), the skill is instantly **Interrupted**, forced onto **Full Cooldown**, and afflicts them with a 1.5s **`"SILENCED!"`** debuff!
* **Split Soul Katana (Soul Slash & True Damage):** Cuts directly into the target's soul, ignoring all damage reduction and shields to deal massive True Damage.
* **Soul Wound Anti-Heal:** Inflicts a 3.0s anti-heal debuff on hit, preventing health regeneration and life-steal.
* **Kinetic Wall Ricochet & 60% Slowdown:** Launches targets at high speed to smoothly bounce off arena walls, afflicting them with a **60% Movement Slowdown for 1.5s** after ricocheting!


### 💥 Ultimate Skill: Curse Inventory - Full Arsenal Unleashed
Toji taps into his Curse Inventory to unleash an inescapable, cinematic execution sequence that pushes his Heavenly Restriction to its absolute limit:
* **Phase 1 (The Swarm):** Toji releases a massive swarm of Fly Heads that visually darkens the arena, blinding the enemy and completely freezing them in fear.
* **Phase 2 (4-Way Assault):** Moving faster than the eye can track, Toji flash-steps to 4 different angles around the frozen target in rapid succession, striking with a different Cursed Tool (and corresponding color flash) each time. Deals immense True Damage.
* **Phase 3 (Crater Slam):** Toji teleports directly above the enemy and brings down a massive final slam that shatters the arena, dealing catastrophic True Damage in a huge blast radius.
